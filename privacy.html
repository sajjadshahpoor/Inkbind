Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

1. The short version

Inkbind's PDF tools run entirely inside your browser. When you merge, edit, or sign a PDF, the file never leaves your device — nothing is uploaded to a server, because Inkbind doesn't have one to upload it to. This policy explains, in the detail EU/UK GDPR requires, exactly what little data is involved and what rights you have over it.

2. Who is responsible for your data (data controller)

Inkbind is designed and developed by Sajjad. For any privacy question or request, you can reach out via LinkedIn.

3. What data Inkbind processes

Inkbind's tools (Merge, Edit & Sign) process your PDF files locally, using JavaScript running in your own browser. Your documents are:

  • Never uploaded to Inkbind or any third party
  • Never transmitted over the network
  • Never seen, stored, or logged by us in any form

Because your files never reach a server we control, Inkbind is not able to act as a data controller or processor for the contents of your documents — that responsibility remains entirely with you, on your own device.

4. Local storage & cookies

Inkbind does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics of any kind. The only information saved on your device is stored using your browser's local storage (a technology similar to cookies, also covered by the EU ePrivacy rules), and only for features you explicitly use:

  • Saved signatures & initials — if you tick "Save for reuse" in the signature tool, the image you drew, typed, or uploaded is saved in your browser's local storage so you don't have to recreate it next time. This is optional and only happens if you opt in.
  • Cookie consent choice — a small flag recording whether you've accepted or declined this notice, so we don't ask again on every visit.

None of this data is ever sent to us or anyone else — it lives only in your browser and stays on your device until you clear it (via your browser settings, or the "Start over" / remove options in the app itself).

5. Legal basis for processing

Where local storage is strictly necessary for a feature to work (e.g. remembering your cookie choice), we rely on our legitimate interest in providing that functionality. Where it is optional (e.g. saving a signature for reuse), we rely on your explicit consent, given by ticking the relevant checkbox — which you can withdraw at any time by deleting the saved item or clearing your browser storage.

6. Data retention

Since Inkbind holds no server-side data, there is no retention period on our end. Anything saved locally in your browser persists until you remove it yourself, clear your browsing data, or decline/withdraw consent for optional storage.

7. Your rights under GDPR

If you are in the EU/UK, data protection law gives you the right to:

  • Access the personal data held about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of your data
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority

In practice, since any data Inkbind touches lives only in your own browser's local storage, you already hold and control it directly — you can exercise most of these rights immediately by clearing that storage or using the in-app controls. For anything else, contact us via the link in section 2.

8. Children's privacy

Inkbind is a general-purpose document tool not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
