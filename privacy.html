Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

1. The short version

Most of Inkbind's PDF tools run entirely inside your browser — when you merge, split, convert, sign, or annotate a PDF, the file never leaves your device. One specific feature is the exception: editing text that's already in a PDF sends that file to Inkbind's own server, so it can preserve the document's original fonts instead of guessing. That server is ours (not a third party), holds nothing in memory beyond the length of your request, and is never used for any other tool. This policy explains, in the detail EU/UK GDPR requires, exactly what data is involved in each case and what rights you have over it.

2. Who is responsible for your data (data controller)

Inkbind is designed and developed by Sajjad, who also operates the Inkbind Server used by the existing-text-editing feature described below. For any privacy question or request, you can reach out via LinkedIn.

3. What data Inkbind processes

Inkbind's tools fall into two categories:

  • Browser-only tools — Merge, Split, Convert, Compress, and everything in Edit & Sign except editing pre-existing text (adding new text, signatures, whiteout, highlights, drawings, forms, deleting pages). These process your PDF entirely in JavaScript running on your own device. Your files are never uploaded, never transmitted over the network, and never seen, stored, or logged by us.
  • Editing existing text — when you click on text already in a PDF to change it, the page and the file are sent to Inkbind's own server so it can read the document's real embedded font and reuse it, which a browser can't do reliably on its own. The server processes the file in memory only, returns the edited result, and does not write the file to disk, log its contents, retain it after the request completes, or share it with any third party. This only happens for that one action — every other edit you make stays local and is combined with the server's result on your device before you download.

For browser-only tools, your files never reach a server we control, so Inkbind is not a data controller or processor for that content — responsibility stays with you, on your device. For the existing-text-editing feature, Inkbind's server does transiently process your file as described above, making us a data processor for that specific, narrow flow.

4. Local storage & cookies

Inkbind does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics of any kind. The only information saved on your device is stored using your browser's local storage (a technology similar to cookies, also covered by the EU ePrivacy rules), and only for features you explicitly use:

  • Saved signatures & initials — if you tick "Save for reuse" in the signature tool, the image you drew, typed, or uploaded is saved in your browser's local storage so you don't have to recreate it next time. This is optional and only happens if you opt in.
  • Cookie consent choice — a small flag recording whether you've accepted or declined this notice, so we don't ask again on every visit.

None of this data is ever sent to us or anyone else — it lives only in your browser and stays on your device until you clear it (via your browser settings, or the "Start over" / remove options in the app itself).

5. Legal basis for processing

Where local storage is strictly necessary for a feature to work (e.g. remembering your cookie choice), we rely on our legitimate interest in providing that functionality. Where it is optional (e.g. saving a signature for reuse), we rely on your explicit consent, given by ticking the relevant checkbox — which you can withdraw at any time by deleting the saved item or clearing your browser storage.

6. Data retention

Inkbind's server (used only for editing existing text) does not persist your files: each PDF is held in memory only for the duration of that single request and is discarded immediately after the edited file is returned to your browser — nothing is written to disk or kept afterward. Anything saved locally in your browser persists until you remove it yourself, clear your browsing data, or decline/withdraw consent for optional storage.

7. Your rights under GDPR

If you are in the EU/UK, data protection law gives you the right to:

  • Access the personal data held about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of your data
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority

In practice, since any data Inkbind touches lives only in your own browser's local storage, you already hold and control it directly — you can exercise most of these rights immediately by clearing that storage or using the in-app controls. For anything else, contact us via the link in section 2.

8. Children's privacy

Inkbind is a general-purpose document tool not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
